BIP-110 Supporters Split onto Minority Chain as Bitcoin Mainnet Pulls Ahead

Supporters of BIP-110, a proposal to restrict non-financial data on Bitcoin for one year, have split onto a minority chain while the main network continues to advance. The split follows BIP-110's automatic enforcement clause activating at block 961,632, with miner signalling near 2.6%. A chain split on Bitcoin is a major consensus event that exposes holders to replay attacks and threatens network confidence. This dispute over block space usage could affect how Bitcoin handles inscriptions and similar data in the future, impacting users, miners, and exchanges. Both chains initially accept identical transactions, so selling the forked coins without replay protection can broadcast the same signed transaction on the main chain and spend the seller's real BTC. Bitcoin developer Kevin Loaec warned that large holders could be targeted first, and advised non-experts to leave coins unmoved until the chains are separated.

rss · The Block · Aug 9, 06:05

Background, discussion, and references

Background

BIP-110 is a Bitcoin Improvement Proposal that would keep pictures, text and other non-financial data—such as Ordinals and inscriptions—out of bitcoin transactions for a year, for example by modifying default relay policy for OP_RETURN outputs. Changing Bitcoin's rules normally requires miner agreement, signalled by marking blocks; BIP-110 needed 55% of blocks in a 2,016-block window, but that threshold was not met. The proposal's second route lets BIP-110-compatible nodes reject unmarked blocks from block 961,632, which can cause a split if enough mining power builds a separate branch. Without built-in replay protection, a chain split duplicates balances on both chains and makes replay attacks possible.

CLARITY Act headed for 60-vote Senate showdown in September

Senate Majority Leader John Thune filed cloture on the CLARITY Act (H.R. 3633) on Saturday, setting up a Senate floor vote when lawmakers return on September 14. The procedural move requires 60 votes to advance the digital-asset market-structure bill. This is the furthest the CLARITY Act has advanced in the Senate and will test whether supporters have a viable bipartisan coalition. Passage would create the first federal market-structure framework for digital assets and clarify SEC and CFTC jurisdiction, reshaping crypto regulation in the U.S. Cloture only ends debate and allows the Senate to proceed to consideration; it does not resolve disputes over stablecoin rewards, illicit-finance safeguards, or officials' crypto holdings. A bipartisan proposal under discussion would require the president to divest from crypto businesses. The Senate Banking Committee previously advanced the bill 15-9.

Background, discussion, and references

Background

The CLARITY Act (Digital Asset Market Clarity Act) would establish a federal market structure for digital assets and delineate regulatory responsibilities between the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC). In the Senate, a filibuster can block legislation unless 60 senators vote to invoke cloture, a procedure created in 1917 and modified in 1975 to require three-fifths of the Senate. The bill has faced opposition from the banking lobby, with JPMorgan CEO Jamie Dimon arguing it lacks strong stablecoin protections.

DeepMind's WeatherNext AI Achieves Breakthrough in Cyclone Forecasting

Google DeepMind's WeatherNext AI model achieved state-of-the-art accuracy in forecasting tropical cyclone track, intensity, and wind structure, as published in a new Nature paper. The company is now open-sourcing the model. This marks a major milestone where AI-based weather models outperform classical numerical weather prediction (NWP) in cyclone forecasting, potentially providing an extra day of warning for vulnerable coastal communities. It also highlights the real-world value of specialized AI models beyond chatbots and coding agents. WeatherNext 2 generates forecasts eight times faster with resolution up to one hour, and is trained on a combination of global analysis data and a database of historical tropical cyclones. Many state-of-the-art AI weather models, including GraphCast, are based on multi-scale hierarchical graph neural networks.

hackernews · bhavansig · Aug 8, 17:18 · Discussion

Background, discussion, and references

Background

Traditional numerical weather prediction (NWP) uses mathematical models of the atmosphere run on supercomputers to forecast weather, but these models are computationally expensive and their skill generally extends only about six days. AI-based forecasters like WeatherNext instead learn directly from historical data, achieving comparable or better accuracy at a fraction of the computational cost. The new work focuses specifically on tropical cyclone forecasting, which involves predicting track, intensity, rainfall, and storm surge.

Discussion

Commenters were overwhelmingly positive, with several praising problem-specific AI models as more impactful than another LLM or coding agent. One noted that AI weather models are already outperforming classic NWP while being orders of magnitude more efficient, and another highlighted that the article announces open-sourcing the model.

Brazil Central Bank Orders 24-Hour Hold on Large Crypto Transfers Abroad

Brazil's central bank has issued Resolution BCB No. 584/2026, published August 7, requiring crypto exchanges to delay customer transfers to foreign platforms and self-custody wallets for up to 24 hours. The rule takes effect January 1, 2027 and applies to transfers exceeding $10,000 (including same-day aggregated amounts) and any smaller transactions flagged as risky. This is a significant operational change for crypto exchanges operating in Brazil, adding friction to legitimate user fund flows and increasing compliance costs. It signals a broader trend of regulators using transaction holds to combat crypto-enabled financial fraud, directly affecting Brazilian users and exchange competitiveness. Exchanges may release a transfer before 24 hours if their risk review finds no wrongdoing, but they must document the decision and notify customers when a transaction is placed on hold. The rule also assigns exchanges greater responsibility for assessing risk based on customer, transaction, counterparty, and destination jurisdiction.

rss · CoinDesk · Aug 8, 23:25

Background, discussion, and references

Background

A self-custody wallet, also called a non-custodial wallet, is a cryptocurrency wallet where the user holds complete control over their own private keys, rather than relying on an exchange or third party to safeguard funds. Such wallets let users transfer cryptocurrencies abroad without an intermediary, which can be used to move funds obtained through financial fraud before victims or institutions recover them. Brazil's central bank is implementing this measure to curb that use, but industry groups argue it may impose costs on legitimate users and weaken domestic exchanges.

Triton: Open-Source DirectX 11 Driver Brings GPU Acceleration to QEMU Windows VMs

Developer Osy has released Triton, a new open-source DirectX 11 driver for QEMU that enables hardware-accelerated 3D graphics in Windows virtual machines. The driver leverages Mesa and virglrenderer components and is currently in early testing, with broader availability expected soon. This gives Windows VM users a decent open-source 3D solution for the first time, filling a long-standing gap in QEMU's GPU virtualization stack. It could reduce reliance on proprietary GPU passthrough or closed-source drivers for many virtualization workloads. The driver aims to support Direct3D 11 specifically; DirectX 12 remains unsupported, a limitation shared by Parallels and VMware. It is built on Mesa and virglrenderer, with the project announced by open-source developer 'Osy' and covered by Phoronix.

hackernews · electricant · Aug 8, 21:33 · Discussion

Background, discussion, and references

Background

QEMU is an open-source machine emulator and virtualizer that can run Windows guests, but its default graphics path has historically offered poor 3D performance. GPU virtualization approaches like Intel GVT-g, NVIDIA vGPU, and SR-IOV exist but often require specific hardware or proprietary components. Virglrenderer is a library that lets guest graphics commands be translated for host GPUs, while Mesa provides open-source graphics driver implementations.

Discussion

Commenters welcomed the project, noting it is 'pretty cool' to finally have a decent open 3D solution for Windows VMs. Others pointed out this is at least the third GPU-related project named Triton, and asked why only DX11 is supported when Parallels and VMware also only offer DX11.
