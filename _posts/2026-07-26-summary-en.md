## [Coinbase CEO warns of quantum threat to Bitcoin](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMid0FVX3lxTFBIVDlwSnJkYUQxUk1JZ3dYTEZyaFduMURUVXNSRW5MYlh2bUVpdXc2UDlUaVR0QjhnZ0ZZa0I3QTE5bTJkSGhiWFBJSVJZeDhISERxTC11QWRNbTlNdGRYbXVlUXRncTBEcDJIY0NEeVRIUktqd3lF?oc=5) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong publicly raised concerns about Bitcoin&\#x27;s vulnerability to quantum computing and announced that Coinbase is preparing for quantum security challenges by investigating post-quantum cryptographic solutions. This warning highlights a long-term existential threat to Bitcoin&\#x27;s security, as quantum computers could break the cryptographic foundations that protect user funds. It pressures the entire cryptocurrency industry to accelerate adoption of quantum-resistant algorithms. Quantum computers could potentially break Elliptic Curve Digital Signature Algorithm \(ECDSA\) keys and weaken SHA-256, which underpins Bitcoin&\#x27;s proof-of-work and address hashing. Vulnerable addresses include legacy Pay-to-Public-Key \(P2PK\) scripts and certain multisignature wallets that have exposed public keys.

google\_news · Cryptopolitan · Jul 26, 12:06

**Background**: Bitcoin relies on public-key cryptography \(ECDSA\) to secure transactions and SHA-256 for mining. A sufficiently powerful quantum computer could reverse a public key to derive the private key, allowing theft of funds. Post-quantum cryptography \(e.g., lattice-based, hash-based schemes\) is being researched to replace current algorithms before large-scale quantum computers become viable.

## [Samsung Wallet to Support USDC and Other Stablecoins](https://www.reddit.com/r/CryptoCurrency/comments/1v6cmkj/samsung_wallet_will_add_stablecoin_support/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Samsung Wallet has announced plans to add support for stablecoins, starting with USD Coin \(USDC\), allowing users to store and manage stablecoins directly on their Samsung devices. This integration represents a major milestone for cryptocurrency adoption, as Samsung is a leading smartphone manufacturer with a massive user base. It brings stablecoins—a key entry point for crypto—directly to millions of users’ everyday devices. The update will likely leverage Samsung&\#x27;s hardware security features such as Knox. While USDC is confirmed, additional stablecoins may be added later, though specific timelines and full list are not yet disclosed.

reddit · r/CryptoCurrency · /u/Kitchen\_Biscotti\_747 · Jul 26, 00:20

**Background**: Stablecoins are cryptocurrencies designed to maintain a stable value by pegging to a reserve asset like the US dollar. USDC is a fully collateralized stablecoin issued by Circle, widely used in decentralized finance \(DeFi\) and payments. Samsung Wallet already supports select cryptocurrencies and blockchain-based services, making this a natural extension.
