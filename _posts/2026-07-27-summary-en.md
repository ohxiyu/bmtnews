## [US citizen charged after GrapheneOS phone wipe at border](https://www.techspot.com/news/113236-us-prosecutors-charge-atlanta-man-after-grapheneos-phone.html) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A US citizen is facing federal charges after using a duress PIN on his GrapheneOS phone, which automatically wiped the device during a border search at an airport. This marks the first known prosecution involving the use of a duress password at a US border. This case sets a legal precedent for the use of duress PINs and other security features that can automatically destroy data, raising important questions about the balance between individual privacy rights and government authority at borders. It could deter security-conscious users from utilizing such features. The duress PIN on GrapheneOS is designed to wipe the device when entered under coercion, but prosecutors argue the act of wiping constitutes obstruction of justice. The outcome could hinge on whether using a duress PIN is considered an intentional act to destroy evidence.

hackernews · eecc · Jul 27, 06:21 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49063022)

**Background**: GrapheneOS is a security-focused Android-based operating system that includes features like duress PINs to protect users&\#x27; data. A duress PIN is a separate password that, when entered, triggers data deletion instead of unlocking the device. US border authorities have broad search powers, and individuals are generally expected to comply with device searches. This incident highlights the legal risks of using anti-forensic tools at borders.
