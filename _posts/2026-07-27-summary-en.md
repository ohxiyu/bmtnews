## [Sberbank to launch crypto trading infrastructure by December](https://cointelegraph.com/news/russias-sberbank-to-launch-crypto-trading-infrastructure-this-year?utm_source=rss_feed&amp;utm_medium=rss&amp;utm_campaign=rss_partner_inbound) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Russia&\#x27;s largest bank, Sberbank, announced plans to launch cryptocurrency trading infrastructure by December 1, coinciding with new Russian crypto regulations that will allow crypto use in foreign trade. This move signals growing institutional adoption in Russia and provides a regulated gateway for crypto trading, potentially boosting the crypto ecosystem in the region. Sberbank is the largest bank in Russia, and the infrastructure is to be operational by December 1, 2023, as Russia establishes market rules and permits crypto for foreign trade.
