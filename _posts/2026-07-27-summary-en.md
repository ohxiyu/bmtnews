## [CFTC Issues Second Warning to Prediction Markets on Self-Certifications](https://cointelegraph.com/news/cftc-issues-second-warning-to-prediction-markets-on-cookie-cutter-self-certifications?utm_source=rss_feed&amp;utm_medium=rss&amp;utm_campaign=rss_partner_inbound) ⭐️ 7.0/10

On July 24, 2026, the CFTC issued an advisory warning prediction markets against using overly broad, template-style self-certifications for event contracts, marking the second such warning this year. This signals increasing regulatory scrutiny of prediction markets, potentially impacting how platforms like Polymarket and Kalshi list event contracts. Compliance costs may rise, and some contracts may be forced off exchanges. The advisory specifically targets &\#x27;cookie-cutter&\#x27; certifications that bundle multiple contract permutations with different settlement sources or methodologies into a single filing. This follows a broader CFTC proposed rulemaking in May 2024 to clarify which event contracts are contrary to the public interest.

rss · Cointelegraph · Jul 27, 01:45

**Background**: Prediction markets are platforms where users trade contracts based on the outcome of future events. Self-certification allows designated contract markets \(DCMs\) to list contracts by certifying they comply with the Commodity Exchange Act, without prior CFTC approval. The CFTC has authority under CEA section 5c\(c\)\(5\)\(C\) to prohibit event contracts it deems contrary to the public interest, particularly those involving gaming or illegal activity.
