## Rubric adjustments

- **Cluster and deduplicate same-event coverage before scoring.** When a second story covers the same event as an already-scored story, cap the duplicate at ≤7.0 or merge it into the original entry. Apply this to the five Tether/KPMG write-ups and the identical `币安因欧盟对俄制裁屏蔽HTX` / `币安将根据欧盟对俄制裁，封锁与HTX等11家交易所之间` pair.
- **Require primary-source confirmation for any score ≥8.0.** Unconfirmed or single-secondary-source reports — e.g., `Coinbase的Base弃用Optimistic Rollups` — get capped at 7.5 until an official announcement lands.
- **Cap opinion essays, research blog posts, and routine vendor releases at 7.5** unless they trigger a confirmed downstream event (regulatory action, product launch, exploit wave). This demotes the Anthropic multi-agent papers, the Matthew Green essay, the Roboflow benchmark, and the DuckDB preview.
- **Score security incidents for wave potential, not just dollar amount.** When an exploit hits, check whether the underlying pattern (shared infrastructure, same attacker, similar vector) connects to recent incidents; if so, raise the score and flag for follow-up. The `Bedrock` 7.0 → `Maya` escalation is the direct failure case.
