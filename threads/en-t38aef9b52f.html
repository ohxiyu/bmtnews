    AI Router OpenRouter Joins Stripe in Reported $7B+ Deal

    OpenRouter officially announced it is joining Stripe, confirming earlier reports that the payments giant would acquire the AI model routing platform for $7B+. The announcement follows a widely discussed acquisition story that had already generated significant community attention. This acquisition brings one of the most widely used AI model routing platforms under the umbrella of a major payments infrastructure company, with significant implications for AI developers and the broader AI tooling ecosystem. It also signals continued consolidation in the AI infrastructure layer as demand for unified model access grows. OpenRouter provides developers with a unified API and marketplace spanning hundreds of AI models from multiple providers, with automatic routing and fallback features. The reported valuation of over $7 billion was not officially confirmed in the announcement itself, which linked back to earlier acquisition reports.

    Stripe Clinches Over $7B Deal to Buy AI Gateway OpenRouter

    Stripe has clinched a deal to acquire OpenRouter, a unified AI model gateway, for over $7 billion. The acquisition merges AI model routing with payment infrastructure. The deal consolidates how developers access and pay for large language models, putting a major payments company at the center of AI infrastructure. It signals that AI gateway services are becoming strategic, multi-billion-dollar infrastructure in their own right. OpenRouter provides a single API endpoint that routes requests to hundreds of models from providers including Anthropic, Google, Meta, and Mistral, with automatic fallbacks and cost-efficient selection. The company was valued at $1.3 billion only months ago, making the reported $7 billion exit a dramatic step-up for its investors.
