    Vitalik Buterin Proposes New Ethereum Roadmap with Quantum Defense and AI Verification

    Vitalik Buterin has proposed a revamped Ethereum roadmap that adds post-quantum defense, AI-based verification, and a potential shift away from the Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM). This is a proposal, not an implemented upgrade. Ethereum is the largest smart contract platform, so a change in its technical direction could affect developers, dApps, Layer 2 networks, and the broader crypto ecosystem. If adopted, the roadmap would represent a major architectural shift in how Ethereum secures and executes code. The proposal reportedly includes preparing Ethereum's cryptography for quantum computing threats and using AI to help verify code correctness. Moving away from the EVM would be a significant break from the current execution environment used by thousands of dApps and rollups.

    Ethereum's latest roadmap prioritizes privacy and quantum resistance, says Buterin

    Ethereum co-founder Vitalik Buterin published an updated roadmap called the "Strawmap" on Monday, elevating privacy, quantum resistance, and AI-assisted formal verification to top priorities. These new focus areas—including post-quantum scaling and native rollups—were not present in his 2023 roadmap. This signals a major strategic shift for Ethereum as it prepares for "Q-Day" and the potential threat of quantum computers, and it could reshape how the network handles privacy and scaling. Because Buterin is a core figure, this roadmap helps align the ecosystem's long-term research and development priorities, potentially affecting developers, validators, and users. The "Strawmap" deprioritizes VDFs and most EVM improvement work, and drops Verkle trees and state expiry in favor of new constructions. Buterin noted that Ethereum is increasingly relying on STARKs and AI-assisted formal verification to ensure protocol correctness, and he reiterated his "Lean Ethereum" vision of a quantum-safe, privacy-first, censorship-resistant, and performant protocol.
